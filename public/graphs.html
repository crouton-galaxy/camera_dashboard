Speed Cameras & Victorian Suburbs

A series of charts exploring the relationship between speed camera deployment and suburb characteristics — income, schools, crashes, transit access, and more. Data from Vic Gov open data and ABS 2021 Census.

01
Cameras by Median Income Bracket
Are cameras evenly distributed across income levels, or concentrated in wealthier / poorer suburbs?
02
Cameras by Number of Schools
Do suburbs with more schools have more cameras? School zones are a common deployment rationale.
03
Cameras by Crash Count
If cameras were deployed reactively to crash history, we'd expect a strong upward trend here.
04
Cameras vs Public Transit Infrastructure
Four panels — metro lines (with V/Line overlay), tram stops, bus routes, and V/Line access. Does transit-rich = camera-rich?
05
Cameras vs Car-Free Households
Suburbs with more households without a car may have different camera needs. How does deployment track this?
06
Camera Type Mix by Income Bracket
Mobile vs DDS vs Fixed — does the type of enforcement infrastructure vary with suburb income?
07
Schools vs Crashes
Independent of cameras — do suburbs with more schools also have more crashes? Dot size = suburb population.
08
Camera Density vs Population Size
Do larger suburbs get proportionally more cameras, or does density thin out as population grows?
